## India CPI Inflation Analysis

### 📊 Project Overview

The project analyzes CPI movements across major consumption categories and uses Excel-based data analysis techniques to identify inflation trends, category contributions, and key economic patterns.

### 🔍 Key Analysis Performed

- CPI Category Contribution: Analyzed the contribution of major categories such as Food & Beverages, Clothing & Footwear, Housing, Health, Transportation & Communication, Education, and Recreation & Miscellaneous.
- Year-over-Year Inflation: Studied the YoY increase in the overall CPI basket for the Rural+Urban sector.
- Month-over-Month Food Inflation: Analyzed monthly movements in food prices across Rural, Urban, and Rural+Urban sectors.
- COVID-19 Impact: Compared CPI trends before and after the onset of COVID-19, focusing on Healthcare, Food, and Essential Services.
- Crude Oil & Inflation: Examined the relationship between imported crude oil prices and inflation across major CPI categories.

### 🛠️ Tools & Techniques

- Microsoft Excel
- Data Cleaning & Transformation
- Data Aggregation
- Category-wise Analysis
- YoY & MoM Calculations
- Pivot Tables
- Charts & Data Visualization
- Trend Analysis
- Comparative Analysis
